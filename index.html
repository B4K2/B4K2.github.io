Preprint // Mechanistic Interpretability

Architectural Inductive Biases Under Extreme Capacity Constraints: A Mechanistic Case Study of 60M-Parameter Language Models

PDF ↗ Code ↗

Trained twenty-four ~60M parameter GPT-2 style models on 1B FineWeb-Edu tokens under a single-axis protocol to isolate why mechanisms designed for 7B–70B models break when compressed.

  • MLA Rank Bound (Prop. 3): Proved that MLA's deficit at r=64 (PPL 33.84) is rank saturation (6–8% of peak singular value at ceiling). Widening to r=256 recovered -1.94 PPL, and adding a decoupled-RoPE branch (M29) reached 31.22 PPL at 2.7× lower KV cache than MHA.
  • Muon on GQA Operating Point (Prop. 1): Plain Muon on GQA yielded a null (32.10 → 32.09). Deriving shape-aware update scaling paired with retuned lr=0.01 (M28) unlocked 31.08 PPL (-1.01 PPL gain).
  • KDA Finite-State Recall Bound (Prop. 2): Proved linear attention trades exact recall for fixed-size states; LAMBADA last-word recall collapsed to 0.116 while local grammar (BLiMP) remained top-tier at 0.782.
  • Perplexity ≠ Capability Divergence: Proved shared-expert MoE models (M14–M16) lead on downstream benchmarks (PIQA 0.608, ARC-Easy 0.485) despite worse single-corpus perplexity.
